Secret Santa ✦

Gift wisely. Wrap nicely. Tell no one.

Somewhere in this office, a colleague is about to make your Christmas a little merrier. Enter your personal code to find out whose Christmas you get to brighten.

How it works

  • Nobody gets their own name.
  • Your result stays the same each time you enter your code, so you can come back to check it.

Ground rules

  • The minimum gift value is ₦7,000. Gifts may exceed this amount at the giver's discretion.
  • Each gift should include a personalised note to the recipient.
  • Participants should keep the identity of their assigned recipient confidential until the reveal.

Prohibited gifts

  1. Cigarettes, vapes or other tobacco/nicotine products.
  2. Offensive, sexually explicit or otherwise inappropriate items.
  3. Items containing discriminatory, political or religiously provocative messaging.
  4. Used or visibly second-hand personal items.
  5. Items intended to embarrass, ridicule or offend the recipient, including prank or joke gifts such as tissues, onions, mugs, Maggi/seasoning products or similar items intended to mock the recipient.
  6. Medication or medical products.
  7. Items that could reasonably be considered unsafe or inappropriate for the workplace, including knives or other potentially dangerous items.

NB: Where a participant is unsure whether a proposed gift is appropriate, they should seek guidance from HR/Admin before purchasing it.
